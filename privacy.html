Privacy

Your information, handled with care

This notice explains what the TANITY Global Med Comms website collects, why we use it, and the choices available to you.

Last updated: 21 August 2026

At a glance

Website enquiries only

This notice covers personal information collected through tanityglobal.com. A client engagement may be governed by additional confidentiality, data-processing, or contractual terms.

Please keep initial enquiries general

Do not submit patient-identifiable data, health records, unpublished datasets, passwords, or other highly sensitive research information through a website form. We can agree on an appropriate transfer method after first contact.

Who is responsible

TANITY Global Med Comms, based in Johannesburg, is responsible for the enquiry information it receives through this website. Privacy questions may be sent to medcomms@tanityglobal.com or discussed on +27-73-098-5588.

Information we collect

Depending on the form you use, this may include your name, email address, phone number, academic level, field, institution, requested support, deadline, and the project details or message you choose to provide.

How we use it

We use enquiry details to respond, understand the requested scope, arrange a consultation, manage follow-up, keep appropriate business records, protect our services, and meet applicable legal obligations. We do not sell enquiry details.

How long we keep it

Retention varies with the nature of an enquiry and any resulting relationship. We keep information only while reasonably needed for the purposes described above, legitimate record-keeping, dispute handling, or legal requirements, then delete or de-identify it when appropriate.

Service providers

Where form information goes

Our contact and consultation forms use Formspree to accept a submission and deliver it to us. Formspree may process technical and submission information in the United States and other countries where it operates. Its current practices and choices are described in the Formspree Privacy Policy ↗.

The site also requests typefaces from Google Fonts. Google may receive technical request information such as an IP address when a font is loaded. See Google's Privacy Policy ↗.

The current website does not set its own analytics or advertising cookies. External websites you choose to visit apply their own policies.

Safeguards

Confidentiality by design

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards appropriate to the information and the service. No internet transmission is completely risk-free, so website forms are not a secure research-data portal.

Access to enquiry information is limited to people and service providers who need it for the purposes described in this notice. Additional safeguards, including NDAs and agreed transfer methods, can be discussed for an engagement.

Your choices

Ask, correct, or object

Subject to applicable law and any information we must retain, you may ask what personal information we hold about you, request access or correction, ask for deletion, withdraw a consent where processing relies on it, or object to certain processing.

Email medcomms@tanityglobal.com with “Privacy request” in the subject line. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

South African privacy enquiries

You may also raise a concern with the Information Regulator South Africa ↗. Its website provides current POPIA guidance, complaint channels, and contact details.

This notice may change when our website, service providers, or legal obligations change. The updated date above will show the latest revision.

Contact us before sharing any project details you consider sensitive.
