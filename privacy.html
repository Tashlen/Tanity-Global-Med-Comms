Website privacy notice

How we handle the information you share when you enquire about our services.

Updated 23 November 2026

In this notice

  • What this notice covers
  • What we collect and why
  • Forms and service providers
  • Retention and safeguards

What this notice covers

This notice covers personal information collected through tanityglobal.com. Additional confidentiality, data-processing or contractual terms may apply to a client engagement.

Keep initial enquiries general. Please leave out patient-identifiable information, health records, unpublished datasets, passwords and other highly sensitive material. Contact us before sharing sensitive project details so we can agree on a suitable transfer method.

TANITY Global Med Comms, based in South Africa, is responsible for the enquiry information it receives through this website. Contact medcomms@tanityglobal.com or call +27 73 098 5588 with privacy questions.

What we collect and why

Depending on the form, this may include your name, email address, phone number, academic level, field, institution, requested support, deadline and the project details or message you choose to provide.

We use these details to respond, understand the requested scope, arrange a consultation, manage follow-up, keep appropriate business records, protect our services and meet applicable legal obligations. We do not sell enquiry details.

Forms and service providers

When you submit a form using its send button, Formspree processes the submission for delivery to us. It may process technical and submission information in the United States and other countries where it operates. Its practices are described in the Formspree Privacy Policy.

If you choose to email us directly, your email application and provider handle your message. Choosing “Prepare email draft” in the local file preview creates a draft on your device for you to review and send.

The website serves its typefaces with its own files and does not set its own analytics or advertising cookies. External websites you choose to visit apply their own policies.

Retention and safeguards

Retention depends on the nature of the enquiry and any resulting relationship. We keep information only while reasonably needed for the purposes described above, legitimate record-keeping, dispute handling or legal requirements. We then delete or de-identify it when appropriate.

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards appropriate to the information and service. No internet transmission is completely risk-free. Website forms are unsuitable for transferring sensitive research data.

Access is limited to people and service providers who need the information for the purposes in this notice.

Your choices

Subject to applicable law and any information we must retain, you may ask what personal information we hold about you, request access, correction or deletion, withdraw consent where processing relies on it, or object to certain processing.

Email medcomms@tanityglobal.com with “Privacy request” in the subject line. We may need to verify your identity before acting on your request.

You may also raise a concern with the Information Regulator South Africa. Its website provides current POPIA guidance, complaint channels and contact details.

We may update this notice when the website, its service providers or our legal obligations change. The date above records the latest revision.
