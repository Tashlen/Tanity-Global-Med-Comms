About TANITY

We bring clinical research, biostatistics and medical editing together to support postgraduate and clinical researchers, as well as institutions in South Africa and internationally.

Who you work with

Our PhD-qualified clinical and laboratory researchers review your work and help you develop your study protocol, analysis plan or manuscript.

Biostatisticians and qualitative researchers help you interpret the findings. Medical writers and editors work with you to present them in a thesis chapter, journal article or conference poster.

How we work together

Tell us which part of your project needs support, along with any deadlines or institutional requirements. We use that information to prepare a quote outlining the work, timing and fees.

We begin work on your protocol, analysis or manuscript once you accept the scope and fees in writing. You make the final decisions and retain authorship.
