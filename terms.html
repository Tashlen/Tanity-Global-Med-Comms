Terms and conditions

These terms describe our services and the commitments we make when we agree to work together.

Scope of services

We provide coaching, structural feedback, methodological guidance, editorial review, and communication strategy. We do not guarantee outcomes and do not provide ghostwriting of theses or manuscripts.

Authorship

Researchers retain authorship and responsibility for submitted work. Our role is advisory and editorial.

Confidentiality

We protect sensitive data and use secure channels for file exchange.

Timelines

Turnaround times are agreed per engagement. Delays due to incomplete inputs will be communicated promptly.

Fees & invoicing

Quotes are scoped by complexity and timelines. Work commences upon written acceptance of scope and fees.

Quality & ethics

We align with institutional integrity policies and international reporting standards. We reserve the right to decline work that conflicts with ethical guidelines.
