Complaints process

If you have a concern about our service, you can ask for a review. We commit to timely acknowledgement and resolution.

  1. Email your concern

    Write to medcomms@tanityglobal.com with your project reference and a description of your concern.

  2. Receive an acknowledgement

    We acknowledge your message within one business day and assign a contact responsible for your complaint.

  3. Review the proposed resolution

    We investigate with the consulting team, propose a remedy and aim to resolve the issue within seven business days.

  4. Request a further review

    If the matter remains unresolved, you can escalate it to our lead consultant for independent review. You will receive a final written response.

Our commitment

We document each step, communicate openly and respect confidentiality and data security throughout the process. Options can include adjusting the scope or clarifying our advice. You can raise a concern without retaliation.
