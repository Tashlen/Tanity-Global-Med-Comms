Expertise / Methods & analysis

Quantitative & statistical analysis.

Work with a statistician to connect your research question to an analysis you can reproduce and explain.

How we can help

  • Review data cleaning decisions, code and the structure of your dataset.
  • Select models, check assumptions and consider alternative analyses.
  • Interpret effect sizes, confidence intervals and p-values in context.
  • Prepare tables and figures that communicate estimates and limitations.

Common questions

Which software do you work with?

We support R, Stata, SPSS and Python. We agree on the tools and outputs at the beginning of the project.

Can we run the analysis together?

Yes. We can review your code or work through an analysis with you, including the checks behind each decision.

Who it’s for

Clinical and postgraduate researchers and their teams analysing observational or experimental data or preparing a quantitative results chapter.

What you’ll receive

Annotated analysis guidance, interpretation notes and repeatable steps for reporting your results.
