Expertise / Writing & publication

Similarity report review.

Read a similarity report in context and decide which matches need attention to quotation, attribution or wording.

How we can help

  • Interpret match types, percentages and institutional guidance.
  • Distinguish correctly attributed material from text that needs revision.
  • Review quotation marks, references and paraphrasing in context.
  • Plan revisions you can make while preserving meaning and attribution.

Common questions

What should I bring?

Bring the report and the corresponding document so that matched passages can be read in context.

Can you promise a particular score?

No. The review focuses on accurate attribution and responsible writing. You make the revisions and retain authorship.

Who it’s for

Researchers and supervisors reviewing a Turnitin or iThenticate report for a thesis, article or abstract.

What you’ll receive

An annotated review and revision checklist with examples of how to handle different kinds of overlap.
