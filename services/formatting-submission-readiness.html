Expertise / Writing & publication

Formatting & submission readiness.

Bring your final manuscript or dissertation into line with the journal or institution’s submission instructions.

How we can help

  • Apply word limits, template styles and document structure.
  • Check references, numbering, figure resolution, captions and table placement.
  • Organise front matter, appendices and required declarations.
  • Prepare supporting files, cover-letter materials and a submission checklist.

Common questions

Who completes the actual submission?

You submit your work through the journal or institutional system. We help prepare and organise the materials.

Can you work with my reference manager?

Yes. We can tidy formatting and metadata for EndNote, Zotero or manually maintained references.

Who it’s for

Authors preparing a final dissertation or journal submission, especially where multiple contributors, figures or file types are involved.

What you’ll receive

A set of final files and a checklist matched to the destination, typically including Word, PDF or figure files as required.
