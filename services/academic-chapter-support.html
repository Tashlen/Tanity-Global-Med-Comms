Expertise / Writing & publication

Academic chapter support.

Use feedback on a chapter draft to connect the literature, methods, results and discussion to your central research question.

How we can help

  • Organise literature around an argument and show where the study fits.
  • Connect the methods chapter to your aims and actual procedures.
  • Choose where text, tables or figures explain results most clearly.
  • Develop the discussion, limitations and implications from the evidence.

Common questions

Can we use my supervisor’s feedback?

Yes. We work through their comments and help you decide where and how to address each point.

Can you match my citation style?

Yes. We follow the institutional style you specify, including APA, Harvard or Vancouver.

Who it’s for

Postgraduate authors revising a dissertation or thesis, supervisors guiding candidates and teams aligning contributions to a shared chapter.

What you’ll receive

An annotated draft, a summary of priority revisions and a checklist for bringing chapters together.
