Expertise / Writing & publication

Editing, proofreading & language review.

Give your manuscript a focused editorial review for its intended readers, from sentence-level clarity to consistency across sections.

How we can help

  • Edit sentences and paragraphs for clarity, coherence and flow.
  • Proofread grammar, punctuation, spelling and terminology.
  • Align the language with your institution, target journal or plain-language audience.
  • Cross-check citation formatting and reference-list consistency.

Common questions

Can you follow a specific spelling convention?

Yes. We use the UK or US spelling and house style required by your publication or institution.

What does the reference check cover?

We check formatting and consistency. The author verifies the source content and its use in the manuscript.

Who it’s for

Academic authors preparing a thesis, article or report, including authors writing in a second language and teams with multiple contributors.

What you’ll receive

A tracked draft, a clean copy and an editorial memo that explains the main changes.
