Expertise / Research planning

Research topic development.

Work through your topic with a research mentor until you can explain what you want to study, why it matters and what your setting allows.

How we can help

  • Identify gaps in the literature and explain what your study could contribute.
  • Narrow the population, setting and scope of the question.
  • Test the idea against available time, resources and ethics requirements.
  • Refine the aims or hypotheses you will take to your supervisor.

Common questions

Who chooses the final topic?

You choose and justify the topic. We help you weigh options and prepare to discuss them with your supervisor.

How long does topic development take?

Most topic reviews take one to two weeks. The timing depends on the scope and the number of feedback rounds.

Who it’s for

Postgraduate and early-career researchers choosing a topic, clinical researchers exploring a question from practice, and departments guiding a cohort.

What you’ll receive

A concise topic brief with your proposed aims, feasibility considerations and starting points for further reading.
