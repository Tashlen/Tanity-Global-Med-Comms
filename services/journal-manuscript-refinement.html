Expertise / Writing & publication

Journal manuscript refinement & editing.

Turn reviewer comments into a manageable revision plan and make it easy for the editor to see what changed.

How we can help

  • Prioritise major and minor revisions and assign responsibilities.
  • Prepare a point-by-point response and a record of manuscript changes.
  • Revisit arguments, limitations and implications in the discussion.
  • Refine language, tables and figures in light of reviewers’ comments.

Common questions

Can you help word a response to a reviewer?

Yes. We can develop a response with you and identify the evidence behind it. You approve the final wording.

What if a reviewer asks for further analysis?

We advise on clarifications to the analysis. We agree a separate scope for substantive re-analysis.

Who it’s for

Authors responding to peer review, revising an article or clarifying the interpretation and implications of their findings.

What you’ll receive

Tracked and clean manuscript files, together with a response matrix linking comments to the relevant changes.
