Expertise / Methods & analysis

Data collection guidance.

Give everyone collecting data a clear account of what to record, how to record it and when to flag a problem.

How we can help

  • Develop case report forms and practical standard operating procedures.
  • Outline consent processes and participant communications for ethics review.
  • Plan collector training and checks for incomplete or inconsistent records.
  • Define data access, secure storage and monitoring responsibilities.

Common questions

Can you advise on REDCap or ODK forms?

Yes. We review form logic and validation checks. If you need construction of the forms, we can agree that scope separately.

Do you provide consent forms?

We share outlines and key clauses. Your team adapts them to its ethics committee’s templates.

Who it’s for

Fieldwork and clinical teams, research assistants and postgraduate researchers, particularly those collecting data across sites or raters.

What you’ll receive

Fieldwork procedures, training notes and checklists adapted to your sites and their shared data quality requirements.
