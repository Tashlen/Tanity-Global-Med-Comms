Expertise / Methods & analysis

Study & methodology design.

Build a study design you can carry out in your setting and explain in your protocol or methods chapter.

How we can help

  • Compare candidate designs against the question, population and resources.
  • Plan sampling and review the assumptions behind a sample size calculation.
  • Select measurement tools and consider validation and licensing.
  • Identify sources of bias, data quality checks and relevant reporting guidance.

Common questions

Can you review a power calculation?

Yes. We agree the inputs with you, review the calculation and help explain its assumptions.

Can the design combine qualitative and quantitative methods?

Yes. We help plan how the two strands fit together, including how one phase informs the next.

Who it’s for

Students and teams designing observational studies, service evaluations, mixed-methods projects or work across multiple sites.

What you’ll receive

A structured methodology plan and an implementation checklist covering your design choices and data management.
