Expertise / Research planning

Research proposal structuring.

Shape a proposal that shows your supervisor or review committee how the question, study design and practical plan fit together.

How we can help

  • Bring the problem statement, objectives and questions into alignment.
  • Explain the choice of methods and the practical steps of the study.
  • Account for governance, ethical risks, resources and timelines.
  • Adapt the proposal outline, budget and appendices to institutional requirements.

Common questions

Who writes and submits the proposal?

You write the proposal and submit it to the relevant institution. We help structure and edit drafts and advise on supporting documents.

Can we work through committee feedback?

Yes. We review the comments with you and help organise responses and revisions.

Who it’s for

Researchers developing a postgraduate, clinical or grant proposal, including teams agreeing on the scope before data collection.

What you’ll receive

An institution-specific outline, feedback on your draft and a checklist for your submission.
