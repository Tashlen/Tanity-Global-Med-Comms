Expertise / Methods & analysis

Qualitative data interpretation.

Trace your analysis from interviews, field notes or focus groups through to themes and the evidence you use to explain them.

How we can help

  • Choose a thematic, content or framework approach suited to the question.
  • Develop a codebook and help coders resolve differences in interpretation.
  • Plan credibility checks and document researcher perspectives and decisions.
  • Select de-identified quotations and report findings using relevant SRQR or COREQ guidance.

Common questions

Who codes the transcripts?

Your research team carries out the primary coding. We guide and review the process using tools such as NVivo, ATLAS.ti or Excel.

Can you advise on bilingual data?

Yes. We can help plan translation checks and discuss dual coding where feasible.

Who it’s for

Qualitative and mixed-methods researchers working with interviews, observations, focus groups or accounts of patient experience.

What you’ll receive

Feedback on your codebook, thematic map and reporting outline, with practical checks for credibility.
