Expertise / Methods & analysis

Systematic reviews & meta-analyses.

Build a documented route from a review question to a set of studies you can appraise, synthesise and report.

How we can help

  • Define eligibility criteria and plan protocol registration where appropriate.
  • Develop database and grey literature search strategies.
  • Pilot screening, extraction forms and risk-of-bias assessments.
  • Choose a synthesis approach and plan how to report it using PRISMA.

Common questions

Who runs the database searches?

We design and review search strategies. You or your librarian run them using your authorised database access.

Can you help with a meta-analysis?

Yes. We discuss whether pooling is appropriate, review effect measures and model choices, and work through heterogeneity checks with you.

Who it’s for

Researchers planning a systematic review or protocol registration, clinical researchers comparing published evidence and teams coordinating several reviewers.

What you’ll receive

A synthesis outline and reporting templates, with protocol, search and screening materials tailored to your review.
